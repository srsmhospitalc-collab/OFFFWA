1. Bukhar Aane Par Kya Kare? - Poori Jankari

Bukhar koi bimari nahi hai, balki sharir ka ek sanket hai ki sharir ke andar koi infection se lad raha hai. Sadharan bukhar aksar viral infection, sardi, ya pani ki kami se hota hai. Agar bukhar 100 se 102 degree tak hai aur 2-3 din me kam ho raha hai, to ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Lakshan:

  • Sir dard, sharir me dard, thakan
  • Thand lagna ya pasina aana
  • Bhookh kam lagna

Kya Kare:

Khoob pani piye, nariyal pani, ORS le. Halka khana khaye jaise daliya, khichdi. Aaram kare. Bina doctor ki salah ke zyada antibiotic na le. Agar bukhar 3 din se zyada rahe, 103 se upar jaye, ulti ya saans me dikkat ho to turant hospital jaye.

Bachav: Saaf pani piye, machhar se bache, haath dhote rahe.

2. Diabetes (Sugar) Ko Control Kaise Kare?

Diabetes aaj kal har ghar ki samasya ban gayi hai. Isme khoon me sugar ka level badh jata hai. Iske mukhya karan galat khan-paan, kam mehnat, motapa aur tanaav hai. Sugar ko control karna mushkil nahi hai, bas roz ki aadat sudharni hai.

Sugar Ke Lakshan:

  • Baar-baar peshab aana, zyada pyaas lagna
  • Thakan, wajan kam hona, ghaav der se bharna

Control Ke Upay:

Subah 30 minute tez chale. Cheeni, mithai, cold drink band kare. Roti me gehu ke saath jowar-bajra milaye. Hari sabzi, dal, salad zyada khaye. Time par khana khaye aur doctor se sugar ki jaanch karate rahe. Dawai apne man se band na kare.

3. Blood Pressure (BP) - High Aur Low BP Ki Pehchan

Blood Pressure ka matlab hai khoon ka naso par dabav. Normal BP 120/80 mana jata hai. High BP ko silent killer kehte hai kyunki iske lakshan der se dikhte hai. Low BP me chakkar aur kamzori aati hai.

High BP se bachne ke liye: Namak kam khaye, tala-bhuna kam kare, gussa aur tanaav kam kare, roz walk kare, dhumrapan aur sharab se door rahe. 40 saal ke baad har 3 mahine me BP check karana chahiye. Agar BP ki dawai chal rahi hai to use roz samay par le.

4. Pet Ki Bimari, Gas Aur Kabz Ka Ilaaj

Pet ki samasya jaise gas, acidity, kabz aaj sabse aam hai. Iska bada karan der se khana, bahar ka khana, kam pani peena aur raat me der tak jaagna hai. Pet saaf nahi hoga to poora din kharab jayega.

Upay: Subah uthte hi 2 glass gunguna pani piye. Khane me fiber wali cheeze jaise palak, papita, kela, daliya shamil kare. Khana chaba-chaba kar khaye. Raat ka khana halka aur jaldi khaye. Roz 20 minute walk zaroor kare. Acidity me mirchi-masala aur chai kam kare.

5. Bachcho Ka Swasthya Aur Mausami Bimari Se Bachav

Bachcho me sardi, khansi, bukhar aur pet dard mausam badalne par aam hai. Bachcho ki rog pratirodhak kshamata kam hoti hai isliye unka khaas dhyan rakhna chahiye.

Bachcho ko roz doodh, dal, anda, hari sabzi de. Bahar ka chips, toffee, cold drink kam de. School se aane ke baad haath-pair dhulwaye. Mausam ke hisaab se kapde pehnaye. Teeka (vaccination) samay par lagwaye. Agar bachche ko 2 din se zyada bukhar, ulti ya dast ho to deri na kare, turant doctor ko dikhaye.

6. Mahilao Ka Swasthya - Khoon Ki Kami (Anemia) Aur Kamzori

Bharat me bahut si mahilao me khoon ki kami payi jati hai. Isse thakan, chakkar, baal jhadna, twacha peela padna jaisi samasya hoti hai. Iska mukhya karan poshak khane ki kami hai.

Upay: Palak, methi, chukandar, gud, chana, anar roz khaye. Vitamin C ke liye nimbu, amla le jisse khoon banta hai. Chai khane ke turant baad na piye. Mahilao ko saal me ek baar hemoglobin ki jaanch zaroor karani chahiye. Garbhavastha me doctor ki salah se iron ki goli le.
